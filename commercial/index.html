Risk Assessment

Commercial Water Damage Risk Assessment and Prevention

The law expects owners of commercial buildings to keep their properties in safe condition, known as a duty of care. Hidden moisture, aging plumbing, and inadequate waterproofing can result in sudden failures that cause injuries, property damage, and costly lawsuits. Perth Amboy Restoration provides professional water damage risk assessments using moisture monitoring equipment and thermal imaging technology that evaluate every area's vulnerability to water intrusion. Findings are delivered in writing, with risks identified, priorities assigned, and specific prevention measures recommended.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Owners who keep up regular professional inspections and follow the experts' advice are viewed favorably by insurers and courts. Every commercial property should have an annual moisture assessment, and high-risk areas, including aging plumbing systems, basements, and mechanical rooms, should be inspected more often.
